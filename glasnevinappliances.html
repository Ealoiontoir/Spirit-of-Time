About us

North Dublin's Trusted Appliance Service

Glasnevin Appliance Services provides clients across North Dublin with the very best in professional home appliance repairs and servicing. With over 17 years of professional experience, we have become the go-to appliance repair company for all of Dublin 9 and the surrounding area.

Our team of experienced repairmen can fix just about any home, garden or kitchen appliance. Every repair comes with a full service that helps extend the life of the appliance and minimises the risk of the problem recurring.

For smaller appliances — microwaves, toasters, vacuum cleaners — simply drop in to our repair shop and our team will deal with it as soon as possible. For larger appliances such as washing machines and dishwashers, we offer a convenient home visit service.
