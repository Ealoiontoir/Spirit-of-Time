Lúcás Ó Dathaire

Painting • Repairs • Restoration

The name Ó Dathaire is inspired by old Irish language and the ideas of colour, light, and restoration. It reflects a simple belief: a well cared-for home changes how people feel inside it.

For over 15 years, Lúcás has worked across Dublin providing reliable painting, wall repairs, door repairs, and general handyman services.

No unnecessary complications. No rushed work. Just steady hands, practical experience, and respect for the homes and people behind every door.

Because good work is not only about fixing things. It is about restoring warmth, balance, and life back into a space.
